# Become a sponsor of weapp-tailwindcss

`weapp-tailwindcss` is an open source project licensed under the `MIT` license and is completely free to use.

Maintaining such a function is actually very energy-consuming and does not produce any actual economic benefits.

Of course, I personally don’t care much about financial gains. Who makes me like doing open source?

## Sponsorship methods

Friends who are willing to give a small amount, your reward will be recorded in the [sponsors](https://github.com/sonofmagic/sponsors) warehouse, and a picture will be generated and hung on my blog site.

Friends who have won big prizes can place advertisements on the official website. The specific URL and style are up to you.

Of course, you can also give nothing. It would also be great if everyone adds us on WeChat and makes friends!
